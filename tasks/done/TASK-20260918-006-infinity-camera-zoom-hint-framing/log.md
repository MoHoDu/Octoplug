Zoom's own dynamic-max-zoom-out policy (`RecomputeDynamicMaxOrthographicSize`) is unchanged and remains unlocked-only, per the existing established Hint-framing decision that a new Hint widens the effective *zoom* range separately via `HouseCameraZoomController.RequestHintFraming` when its own full bounds require it. Room Generation's data structures were not touched.
