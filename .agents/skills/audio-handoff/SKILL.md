# Audio Handoff

1. Confirm approved audio direction and Allowed Scope.
2. List source/export files, format, sample rate, channels, duration, loop points, and loudness notes.
3. State category, intended trigger, repetition behavior, and variants.
4. Record unresolved mixing, priority, spatialization, or trigger questions as Open Decisions.
5. Do not replace audio or mutate scenes/prefabs/mixers without Exclusive Assets.
6. Verify files decode correctly and identify any Git LFS requirement.
7. Hand off Unity import/integration to an authorized Unity operator.
