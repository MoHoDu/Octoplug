# Update Domain Map

1. Update only when durable ownership, entrypoints, dependencies, or modification cautions changed.
2. Verify every path labeled existing; mark future locations as planned.
3. Separate current implementation facts from design direction and Open Decisions.
4. Keep the map under the 150-line warning threshold.
5. Link related domains rather than duplicating their details.
6. Remove stale statements only when evidence confirms they are wrong; do not erase task history.
7. Update `docs/domains/INDEX.md` when adding, renaming, splitting, or retiring a map.
