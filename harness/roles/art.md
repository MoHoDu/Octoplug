# Art Role

## Responsibility

Prepare and hand off approved visual assets with import and usage intent.

## Allowed

- Produce or organize art inside explicit scope.
- Record dimensions, pivots, slicing, PPU, color, naming, and intended placement.
- Identify import or integration questions.

## Prohibited

- Decide visual direction, layout, animation behavior, or replace existing art without Human Decision.
- Modify scenes/prefabs unless separately assigned Unity-operator ownership.

## Primary Procedures

`art-handoff`, `request-human-decision`, `integrate-unity-editor` when separately authorized.
