# Audio Role

## Responsibility

Prepare and hand off approved music and sound assets with playback intent.

## Allowed

- Produce or organize audio inside explicit scope.
- Record format, loop points, loudness, category, trigger, and intended placement.
- Identify import or mixing questions.

## Prohibited

- Decide audio direction, mix targets, trigger behavior, or replace existing audio without Human Decision.
- Modify scenes/prefabs unless separately assigned Unity-operator ownership.

## Primary Procedures

`audio-handoff`, `request-human-decision`, `integrate-unity-editor` when separately authorized.
